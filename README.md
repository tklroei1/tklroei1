## Hi, I'm Roei Klein

Product-minded builder exploring AI, automation, and human-centered software. I like turning fuzzy problems into working tools — and thinking carefully about how they should feel to use.

I build across the stack (JavaScript/Node, serverless, browser APIs) and use AI where it earns its place: as assistance, not as an autonomous source of truth.

### Selected work

**Working software**

- **[personal-os](https://github.com/tklroei1/personal-os)** — a personal operations workspace that brings planning, knowledge, and AI-assisted workflows into one browser-based app. Deployed and in daily use. [Live demo](https://personal-os-coral-tau.vercel.app)

**Case study**

- **[voice-hotspot-showcase](https://github.com/tklroei1/voice-hotspot-showcase)** — a public case study of a real-time conversation co-pilot that reads emotion from both words and voice tone, fuses them, and flags where tone contradicts words. The full product is private; this repo explains the engineering and the honest, held-out evaluation behind it. [Read it](https://tklroei1.github.io/voice-hotspot-showcase/)

**Product design exercise**

- **[clarity-mode](https://github.com/tklroei1/clarity-mode)** — a self-contained design exercise exploring a calmer, focus-oriented writing experience. A concept and prototype, not a shipped product.

### How to read this profile

Open to conversations about product, AI, and building things people actually want to use.
