## Hi, I'm Roei Klein

Product-minded builder exploring AI, automation, and human-centered software. I like turning fuzzy problems into working tools — and thinking carefully about how they should feel to use.

I build across the stack (JavaScript/Node, serverless, browser APIs) and use AI where it earns its place: as assistance, not as an autonomous source of truth.

### Selected work

- **[personal-os](https://github.com/tklroei1/personal-os)** — *working prototype.* A personal operations workspace that brings planning, knowledge, and AI-assisted workflows into one browser-based app. [Live demo](https://personal-os-coral-tau.vercel.app)

- **[voice-hotspot-showcase](https://github.com/tklroei1/voice-hotspot-showcase)** — *technical case study.* A real-time conversation co-pilot that reads emotion from both words and voice tone, fuses them, and flags where tone contradicts words. The full product is private; this repo explains the engineering and the honest, held-out evaluation behind it. [Read it](https://tklroei1.github.io/voice-hotspot-showcase/)

- **[clarity-mode](https://github.com/tklroei1/clarity-mode)** — *product design exercise.* A self-contained concept exploring a calmer, focus-oriented writing experience. A prototype, not a shipped product.

### How to read this profile

I try to be clear about what each project *is*: working prototypes, case studies of private work, and design explorations are labeled as such. Numbers I share come from my own testing and are described with their method, not presented as external benchmarks.

Open to conversations about product, AI, and building things people actually want to use.
